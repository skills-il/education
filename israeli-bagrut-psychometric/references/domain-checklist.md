# Domain Coverage Checklist: Bagrut and Psychometric

Anchor list for reviewing this skill's completeness. Scope: navigating the
Bagrut matriculation exams and the Psychometric Entrance Test (PET), grades,
admission scores, and student rights. Category: education.

## Must cover (core)

- Bagrut grade formula: 70% external exam + 30% magen (school grade); 2026 wartime option allows 60% exam / 40% magen when it raises the grade. Source: Ministry of Education tziun beit-sifri circular; gov.il 30/70 page.
- Study units (1-5) and the 21-unit minimum for a full certificate, plus mandatory cores (Hebrew, English 3+ units, math 3+ units, civics/history, Tanakh/literature). Source: edu.gov.il bagrut eligibility.
- Passing grade 55 (45-54 is failing). Source: edu.gov.il eligibility.
- 5-unit university bonus: flat per subject, awarded only at grade 60 or above, amount varies by university (Technion math +30, sciences +25, humanities/English +20; Tel Aviv University math +35). NOT tiered by score. Source: Technion median-grade page; TAU calc page.
- Magen grade: school assessment, 30% of the subject grade and the fallback if a student cannot sit the external exam. Source: tziun beit-sifri circular.
- New-immigrant accommodations and Hebrew-level bonus: +10 (level alef) / +15 (levels bet/gimel); 10 years from aliyah or until age 23 (whichever later); Ethiopian immigrants 12 years (or until age 25). Source: Kol Zchut olim accommodations.
- Learning-disability accommodations on a recognized diagnosis (extra time, oral exam, reader/scribe, separate room). Source: Ministry of Education accommodations rules; NITE parallel accommodations.
- Reserve-duty (miluim) accommodations: special sessions, deferrals, magen substitution; expanded under post-2023 wartime directives. Source: Ministry of Education; reservist rights framework.
- Moed bet / grade improvement: second sitting and re-sit to improve (higher grade counts), available also to graduates/external examinees. Source: edu.gov.il moadim rules.
- Grade appeals (irur): right to appeal a school summary grade and exam-integrity procedures. Source: edu.gov.il appeals procedure.
- PET structure: NITE test; verbal, quantitative, and (through autumn 2026) English.
- December 2026 PET reform: English leaves the psychometric and becomes a separate computerized test (AMIRNET, in NITE's AMIR family); the psychometric becomes two-domain, Quantitative 50% + Verbal 50%; scale stays 200-800; old three-domain scores valid 7 years. Source: NITE two-domain general-information page; TAU; N12/Mako.
- PET scoring: 200-800, mean about 530, SD about 100, section scores 50-150, valid 7 years. Source: NITE.
- PET fee about 665 NIS for 2026 (higher for late/overseas/accommodations). Source: NITE test-dates-and-prices.
- PET languages: Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, French, Spanish, combined. Source: NITE.
- PET registration: online via NITE, fixed national sessions, late-registration surcharge. Source: NITE.
- Sekhem (combined admission score): weighted bagrut average + psychometric, typically about 40% bagrut / 60% psychometric (varies by university and faculty, some scale bagrut by a multiplier). Source: TAU, HUJI, BGU, Haifa formulas.

## Should cover (advanced)

- Field-specific (domain-weighted) psychometric scores so faculties can favor verbal or quantitative.
- Admission tracks beyond sekhem: psychometric-only / above-threshold, mechina (preparatory year), mature applicant, excellence tracks. Source: universities; Council for Higher Education.
- Age-based psychometric exemption (commonly age 27-30+) and exemption for holders of a prior academic degree.
- Foreign-credential recognition (foreign matriculation, equivalence, accepted substitutes).
- How the new standalone English exam feeds university English placement/exemption levels.
- Bonus stacking nuance: caps on total bonus, partial 4-unit bonuses (varies by institution).
- External (extern) bagrut completion and second-chance routes.
- Accommodation documentation timelines and diagnosis validity windows.
- Reserve-duty and wartime university admission leniencies.

## Out of scope (explicit)

- Specific exam content, answer keys, syllabi, or test-prep tutoring strategies.
- Exact per-faculty yearly cutoff numbers (point to official calculators instead).
- Graduate-school admission (GRE, master's entrance) and professional licensing exams.
- College vs university distinctions beyond admission mechanics; tuition and financial aid.
- Non-Israeli standardized tests (SAT, ACT, IELTS, TOEFL) except where an Israeli university accepts them as a substitute.
- High-school course-selection counseling and career guidance.

## Authoritative sources

- Ministry of Education portal (edu.gov.il): bagrut eligibility, accommodations, moadim, appeals.
- Ministry of Education tziun beit-sifri (magen) circular.
- NITE / National Institute for Testing and Evaluation (nite.org.il): structure, scoring, fees, dates, the two-domain reform page.
- Council for Higher Education (che.org.il).
- Kol Zchut (kolzchut.org.il): bagrut rights, accommodations, olim.
- University sekhem pages: Tel Aviv University, Hebrew University, Ben-Gurion University, University of Haifa, Technion, Bar-Ilan University.
