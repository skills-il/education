# Domain Coverage Checklist: Bagrut and Psychometric

Anchor list for reviewing this skill's completeness. Scope: navigating the
Bagrut matriculation exams and the Psychometric Entrance Test (PET), grades,
admission scores, and student rights. Category: education.

## Must cover (core)

- Bagrut grade formula: 70% external exam + 30% magen (school grade) in most subjects; some subjects publish a different split, and where alternative school-based assessment is heavy the school share can exceed 30%. Source: circular Hora'at Keva 0230; Ministry certificate-composition page. NOTE: a "60/40 wartime option" was asserted here through v1.3.2 on the strength of a cms.education.gov.il page; that host no longer resolves and a positive search of circulars 0230 and 0045 and the composition page found no support. Do not reinstate it without a live source.
- Study units (1-5) and the 21-unit minimum for a full certificate, including at least one 5-unit moogbar subject, for which ENGLISH DOES NOT COUNT. Core unit counts by sector: mamlachti 16, mamlachti-dati 20, atzmai/charedi 20, Arab 17, Druze 17. Source: edu.gov.il bagrut eligibility.
- Passing grade 55 (45-54 is failing; 01-04 is a chosem/blocking grade). One failing grade tolerated if not in mother tongue or social involvement. Source: edu.gov.il eligibility.
- 5-unit university bonus, enumerated as a FULL per-institution table rather than a single representative row. Awarded only at grade 60 or above; not tiered by score. Technion: math +30; physics/chemistry/biology/recognized technological +25; literature/Bible/history/English/Arabic/Hebrew-for-Arabic-speakers +25; other listed subjects +20; plus an enlarged science/technology combination branch lifting physics and chemistry to +30 and lowering technological to +20; math units count double in the best average. TAU: math 5 +35; English/physics/chemistry/biology/Arabic/literature/history/Bible 5 +25; English and math at 4 +12.5; other listed subjects 5 +20 and 4 +10; nothing for a combination-of-subjects grade. Source: Technion median-grade page; TAU how-to-calculate page.
- Best (optimal) average: universities drop the subjects that lower the average rather than averaging the whole certificate. Source: TAU worked example (97.81 straight vs 108.39 adapted).
- Magen grade: school assessment, 30% of the subject grade and the fallback if a student cannot sit the external exam. Source: tziun beit-sifri circular.
- New-immigrant accommodations and points bonus. Levels are defined by the GRADE OF ARRIVAL (alef = grades 1-3, bet = 4-8, gimel = 9-12), not by Hebrew proficiency: +10 at level alef, +15 at levels bet and gimel, plus +10 on the external English exam at all three levels except for students from English-official-language countries. Applied to both the school annual grade and the exam grade, only on regular Hebrew-language written papers, and never to the 30% school-assessment component. Duration 10 years from aliyah or until age 23 (whichever later); Ethiopian immigrants 12 years or until age 25. Source: circular Hora'at Keva 0045.
- Learning-disability accommodations on a recognized diagnosis (extra time, oral exam, reader/scribe, separate room). Source: Ministry of Education accommodations rules; NITE parallel accommodations.
- Reserve-duty (miluim) accommodations: special sessions, deferrals, magen substitution; expanded under post-2023 wartime directives. Source: Ministry of Education; reservist rights framework.
- Moed bet / grade improvement: second sitting and re-sit to improve (higher grade counts), available also to graduates/external examinees. Moed bet is a SUMMER-session mechanism only and covers only English, mathematics and mother tongue. Source: edu.gov.il moadim rules; תשפ"ז summer draft timetable.
- Exam-grade appeals (irur): 30 days from publication for school students, 21 days for external examinees; the grade can go down; the scanned booklet may be requested within 10 days. Exam-integrity procedures are separate. Source: edu.gov.il appeals procedure.
- PET structure: NITE test; verbal, quantitative, and (through autumn 2026) English.
- December 2026 PET reform: English leaves the psychometric and becomes a separate computerized test (AMIRNET, in NITE's AMIR family); the psychometric becomes two-domain with EQUAL verbal and quantitative weights, alongside a quantitative-emphasis and a verbal-emphasis score each weighting one domain three times the other; scale stays 200-800; old three-domain scores valid 7 years, with a recomputed two-domain score published by NITE from October 2026. Source: NITE two-domain score-calculation page.
- Institutional treatment of a pre-reform score: at TAU, the higher of the three-domain and recomputed two-domain score (highest of three if the candidate also re-sits), from the תשפ"ח season as a two-year pilot; other institutions decide independently. Source: TAU psychometric-change page.
- PET scoring: general score 200-800, domain scores 50-150, valid AT LEAST 7 years (set by the institutions, not NITE). NITE states most departments require a score close to the average, 550. NITE publishes NO standard deviation and NO percentile band; do not assert one. Source: NITE scores pages.
- PET fee: TWO live tables. Three-domain (through Sept 2026) 665 standard / 1,000 late / 1,665 same-day / 120 date-change. Two-domain (from Dec 2026) 495 / 745 / 1,240 / 89. AMIRNET 315, date change 142. No early-registration discount and no score-reporting fee are published. Source: NITE psychometric and two-domain pages.
- PET languages: Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, French, combined Hebrew-English. NO currently published session offers Spanish (positive search across four NITE pages returned zero hits); Dec 2026 is Hebrew and Arabic only. Source: NITE test-dates table.
- PET registration: online via NITE, fixed national sessions, late-registration surcharge. NITE publishes ONE close date per session (which is also the accommodations-request deadline) and no separate open or late-close date; do not assert deadlines it has not published. Source: NITE test-dates table.
- Sekhem (combined admission score): NO national weighting exists and none should be quoted. TAU states the formula changes yearly and publishes a linear transformation; the Technion publishes per-faculty formulas; HUJI publishes no weighting numbers. Route the user to the institution's own calculator. Source: TAU how-to-calculate; Technion admissions; HUJI reception-components.

- Exam calendar: the תשפ"ז draft winter (27.12.2026-4.2.2027) and summer (25.4-9.7.2027, moed bet 4-8.7.2027) timetables, published in draft 12/07/2026 and expressly subject to change.
- Bagrut registration and accommodation deadlines: winter through 20 November, summer 1 February to 20 March; the accommodation must be realized by that date, and a supporting diagnosis must be under five years old for an examinee over 18.

## Should cover (advanced)

- Field-specific (domain-weighted) psychometric scores so faculties can favor verbal or quantitative.
- Admission tracks beyond sekhem: psychometric-only / above-threshold, mechina (preparatory year), mature applicant, excellence tracks. Source: universities; Council for Higher Education.
- Age-based psychometric exemption and exemption for holders of a prior academic degree. The qualifying age is set per institution; no figure is asserted in the skill because none was verified this cycle. Coverage debt, see optimization-log.json.
- Foreign-credential recognition (foreign matriculation, equivalence, accepted substitutes).
- How the new standalone English exam feeds university English placement/exemption levels. AMIRNET is scored 50-150 and is equivalent to AMIR/AMIRAM and to the psychometric English-domain score; NITE publishes NO patur threshold table and warns that institutions differ.
- Bonus stacking nuance: caps on total bonus, partial 4-unit bonuses (varies by institution).
- External (extern) bagrut completion and second-chance routes.
- Accommodation documentation timelines and diagnosis validity windows.
- Reserve-duty and wartime university admission leniencies.

- Social involvement (מעורבות חברתית): a 180-hour programme over grades 10-12, recorded as a code 1-4 rather than a grade; code 1 means NO certificate. Source: edu.gov.il eligibility page.
- External examinees have no magen: the 70/30 split does not apply to them and their subject grade is composed of questionnaire weights (100%, or 60/40, or 50/30/20). On a re-sit of the same questionnaire the highest score counts. Source: externs takanon (TakanonExterni092025NEW.pdf).
- NITE accommodations are a SEPARATE track from Ministry of Education Bagrut accommodations and do not carry over; the request deadline equals the registration-close date. Source: NITE accommodations section.
- From December 2026 AMIRNET is a separate registration and a separate fee; sitting the two-domain psychometric leaves the candidate with no English score until they book it, with a 35-day minimum between sittings. Source: NITE AMIRNET pages.

## Out of scope (explicit)

- Specific exam content, answer keys, syllabi, or test-prep tutoring strategies.
- Exact per-faculty yearly cutoff numbers (point to official calculators instead).
- Graduate-school admission (GRE, master's entrance) and professional licensing exams.
- College vs university distinctions beyond admission mechanics; tuition and financial aid.
- Non-Israeli standardized tests (SAT, ACT, IELTS, TOEFL) except where an Israeli university accepts them as a substitute.
- High-school course-selection counseling and career guidance.
- Discharged-soldier funding entitlements (the pikadon deposit, IDF-funded bagrut completion, mechinot for released soldiers). Reviewed 2026-08-27 under the re-litigation rule: an ordinary user of this skill plausibly DOES ask about these, so the skill now routes them explicitly (references/university-admission-guide.md tip 7) rather than staying silent. The mechanics themselves are a separate entitlement system under חוק קליטת חיילים משוחררים and are not summarised here, because a wrong eligibility window on a time-limited deposit is worse than a referral. The bagrut-completion path itself IS covered, via the external-examinee route.
- College (michlala) and Open University admission mechanics. Reviewed 2026-08-27: most Israeli undergraduates study at colleges, so silence was a real hole. The skill now names both routes and states that the Open University admits with no bagrut and no psychometric, but does not reproduce their admission rules.
- BGU, Bar-Ilan and Haifa bonus tables: attempted this cycle, sources unreachable (deep links redirected or 404'd). NOT a scope decision, a coverage debt; carried in optimization-log.json for the next cycle.
