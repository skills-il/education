# NITE tests, deadlines and procedures

Everything here is about מאל"ו (NITE) rather than the Ministry of Education.

**Sourcing status, read this first.** The fees in section 4 and the test-family routing in section 5
were read directly off nite.org.il on 2026-08-27 and are carried in `evidence.json`. The retake rules
in section 1, the accommodations timing in section 2 and the re-check deadlines in section 3 are
**NOT independently confirmed against a NITE page in this cycle** and carry no evidence entry. Treat
them as a checklist of the right questions to ask NITE, not as figures to quote to a student. Where
one of them decides a student's action, send them to NITE to confirm. Re-verify the whole file each
cycle: this is the fastest-moving material in the skill.

## 1. Retaking the psychometric

- **Reportedly no limit on the number of attempts. UNCONFIRMED this cycle.** Verify with NITE before relying on it.
- **The prohibition on sitting consecutive dates is reported to have been abolished. UNCONFIRMED this cycle.**
  Students widely believe a spacing rule still exists. Do NOT tell a student the ban is gone on the
  strength of this file alone: if they act on it and it is wrong, they lose a whole sitting. Send them
  to NITE to confirm before they plan around it.
- Every score stays valid for at least 7 years, old three-domain and new two-domain formats alike.
  Institutions generally read the highest, but that is the institution's rule, not NITE's, so check.

## 2. Accommodated testing (תנאים מותאמים)

- The request deadline is the **same date as the registration deadline** for the sitting (this one IS
  confirmed: NITE's calendar shows an identical date in the registration and accommodations columns on
  every row). There is no
  extra window, which is the trap: a student who registers on the closing day has already missed it.
- Accommodated sittings run alongside each regular date or within about three weeks of it.
- Held in Israel only.
- An accommodated candidate sitting in a language other than Hebrew gets **YAELNET** free at a NITE
  centre rather than YAEL immediately after the test.

## 3. Re-checking an answer sheet (בדיקה חוזרת)

- A manual re-scan of the answer sheet. The request window is reported as **3 months** from the test
  date and the result as **7 working days**, both UNCONFIRMED this cycle. Confirm the current windows
  with NITE rather than quoting these.
- Carries a handling fee, refunded if an error is found. NITE does not publish the amount on the
  reevaluation page, so do not quote a figure.
- **The writing task is not re-graded.** Only the machine-scored sheet is re-checked.

## 4. Fees and money

- Psychometric, September 2026 sitting: **665 NIS**. NITE had not published the fee for the
  December 2026, April 2027 or July 2027 sittings. Do not present 665 as the standing fee.
- Late registration: **150 percent** of the regular fee, up to 8 business days before.
- Cancellation: free within 48 hours; 5 percent within 14 days; 15 percent after that but before
  registration closes; 30 percent after closing.
- Date change after closing: **18 percent**.
- **AMIRNET: 315 NIS**, and 142 NIS to change the date.
- No fee-reduction or exemption path was found on NITE's site. This was searched for positively
  rather than assumed absent, and the result is UNVERIFIED, not "there is none". Tell a student who
  needs one to ask NITE directly rather than telling them no such thing exists.

## 5. The rest of the NITE test family

A student is routinely routed to the wrong test because only the psychometric is well known. The
psychometric is not the entrance test for several of the most competitive tracks.

| Test | What it is for |
|---|---|
| מו"ר / מרק"ם | Medical school admission systems. A student who says "I want medicine" needs this, not just a high PET |
| מר"ב | A further medical-admission system |
| מתא"ם | Admission to a master's in psychology |
| יע"ל / יעלנט | Hebrew-knowledge test for non-Hebrew examinees. Free straight after a non-Hebrew PET. Reported separately from the PET score; some institutions make it an admission condition, not merely placement |
| אמירם / אמירנט | English placement, and from December 2026 the sole English measure for higher-education admission, since English leaves the psychometric |
| מימ"ד, מת"ל, מכון אלה, שאו"ל | Further NITE instruments; route by name rather than guessing |

## 6. Score reporting

- Institutions receive scores automatically only if the candidate listed them.
- Forecast reporting dates are published per sitting: September 2026 to 18.10.2026; December 2026
  to 20.1.2027.

## 7. Appeals on the Bagrut side

Distinct from a NITE re-check and often confused with it. A Bagrut grade appeal runs through the
Ministry's ערעור process (the שילובית system), and the school summary grade has its own route. Send
the student to their school and to the Ministry's exams portal; do not describe a NITE re-check
procedure to a student asking about a Bagrut grade.
