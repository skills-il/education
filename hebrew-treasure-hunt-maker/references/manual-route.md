# Hosts that cannot run scripts

claude-ai, chatgpt, manus, grok and claude-desktop without a local tool cannot run `scripts/`. The parent still gets a full kit.

## Honesty rule

**Never say a check ran.** Open the kit with this line, in the parent's language:

> הקובץ נבדק ידנית מול רשימת הבדיקות של הסקיל, בלי הבדיקה האוטומטית. המקומות והפרסים נבחרו מתפריט סגור, אבל בדיקת הטקסט חלקית. לכן חשוב במיוחד שמבוגר יקרא כל כרטיס ויעבור על המסלול לפני המשחק.

Do not write "verified", "validated", "passed" or "safe".

## The manual pass, in this order

This expands Step 6 of SKILL.md. Go through every line and show the parent a short table: item, result, what changed.

1. **Youngest present.** Write the age of the youngest child in the home, guests and toddlers included. If the parent did not rule out younger children, write "a younger child may be present" and treat the youngest as possibly under 3 for prizes. A child of unknown age counts as possibly under 3 for prizes and possibly under 6 for the adult-presence rule.
2. **Each station** is one menu room and one menu spot from SKILL.md Step 2. Replace anything else and tell the parent why, with a suggested alternative. No bed spot unless the parent confirmed the bed is low and not a bunk.
3. **Protected space.** Way clear, nothing breakable added, door out of the game. No mamad station unless the parent confirmed the way is clear and nothing breakable is in it. With a mamad station, the host guide states all three conditions.
4. **Prizes** come from the menu in SKILL.md Step 3 for that youngest child. With anyone under 5, no food. If a child under 3 may be present, only a large book, a large soft toy without small parts or the certificate alone, so chocolate coins, dreidels, stickers and other restricted items are out. No other props, apart from a box or envelope in an escape room.
5. **Route.** Write the chain: start, station 1, station 2, up to the treasure. For each clue, confirm its answer is the next station. No loops, no station left out.
6. **Answer in the riddle.** For each clue, read the text and each of hints 1 and 2 for the answer word: with a prefix (בספה, הספה), in full or short spelling (שולחן / שלחן), inside a longer word (כדורסל for סל), as a synonym (ממ"ד / מרחב מוגן), reversed, or spelled out (מ-מ-ד). Also read every riddle and hint for a place off the menu or a dangerous action, and name the one place it leads to.
7. **Reading level and niqqud** per `reading-stages.md`. Get the niqqud from the Nakdan website (`niqqud-routes.md`, Route B). If the parent prefers not to, set the card to "an adult reads aloud". Never vocalize it yourself.
8. **Gender.** Read every second-person form against the table in `reading-stages.md`.
9. **Calendar and candles.** If the parent gave a date, read it from Hebcal, write the year, and flag a Friday, Saturday or festival. With no date, leave it blank and write no year. Apply `calendar-shabbat.md` only for a family that keeps them. If observance is unknown, follow the Shabbat row of the Step 1 table in SKILL.md. On any date, if candles will be lit, no station or treasure goes in that room or a space open to it, and one adult stays with them while another stays with the children. With no second adult, offer to play before lighting or after the candles are out. If candles are unknown, apply the same check to the living room and dining area, and print on the host guide: "משחקים לפני ההדלקה או אחרי שהנרות כבו." If the date is unknown or in Hanukkah, also print: "לא משחקים בזמן טיגון."
10. **Atbash or scramble.** Decode letter by letter in the reply. If you cannot show the decoding, use a different puzzle.

## The output

If the host can show a document or an HTML page, produce one RTL A4 document. Otherwise give plain text blocks the parent can paste into Word or Google Docs. Keep this order and these rules:

1. Host guide: what was checked by hand, what was not, before you start, the siren line from SKILL.md Step 6 word for word, the rabbi line, candle line, frying line and kids' room mamad line when Step 6 requires them, emergency numbers 101, 102 and 04-7771900, a line telling the adult to read every card before hiding it, headcount and closed-front-door lines, cleanup list, and a Dicta credit if Dicta niqqud was used.
2. Answer sheet: step, where to place it, which cards, the solution. Children never see this page.
3. Hint ladder: hint 1, hint 2, answer and why, for each child and step.
4. Clue cards per child, with a dashed cut line. A card shows the child's name and card number, and **never** the hiding place.
5. One certificate per child, first name only.

Use black and white, no color emoji, and text at least 15pt on a card for a child who reads alone.
