# Ma'on Yom and Mishpachtonim Tuition and Subsidy Tables

**Governing school year: תשפ"ו (September 2025 - August 2026).**

The tariff runs on a **school year**, not a calendar year. As of 19 August 2026 the Ministry
of Labour has published tables for תשפ"ו and תשפ"ה only. **The תשפ"ז (September 2026 -
August 2027) tables are NOT yet published**, even though the source page carries an update
stamp of 03.08.2026. Never present the figures below as "current" without naming תשפ"ו, and
never extrapolate them to תשפ"ז.

Source: <https://www.gov.il/he/pages/tuition-daycare-and-supervised-nurseries?chapterindex=3>
(Ministry of Labour, אגף בכיר לעידוד תעסוקת הורים). All amounts are **NIS per month**.

## How the tables work

A family is assigned a **דרגה (grade)** on a scale of 3 to 15. The grade, not the income
itself, selects the row. Each row gives two numbers per column: **השתתפות הורים** (what the
parent pays) and **השתתפות ממשלה** (what the state pays). The two always sum to the full
tariff for that column.

Grades 11 and 12 receive **no subsidy at all**: the parent pays the full tariff.

## Which of the four ma'on tables applies (ASK, never assume)

There is no single ma'on table. There are **four**, and the correct one depends on two
properties of the specific ma'on the child attends:

1. **Operator type**: מלכ"ר (non-profit) or שאינו מלכ"ר (for-profit).
2. **Staffing standard**: תקינה בסיסית (basic) or תקינה מורחבת (extended).

The spread between the cheapest and most expensive table is large (a 0-15-month place is
3,487 under מלכ"ר בסיסית and 4,310 under שאינו מלכ"ר מורחבת), so picking the wrong table
misstates a parent's bill by hundreds of shekels a month. **Always ask the parent which
operator type and staffing standard their ma'on has** before quoting a figure. If they do
not know, tell them to ask the ma'on directly or call *2969, and give a range across the
four tables rather than a single number.

Mishpachtonim (family daycare) have **one** table with no age dimension.

## Grade-setting rules

Source: Kol Zchut, "דרגות השתתפות במימון מעונות יום ומשפחתונים",
<https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/דרגות_השתתפות_במימון_מעונות_יום_ומשפחתונים>

**Per-capita income (הכנסה לנפש).** Take the parents' average **gross** income and divide by
the number of people in the household (parents plus children up to age 18).

- Two-parent family: sum of both parents' gross income divided by household size.
- Single parent: **half** the parent's gross income, divided by (household size + 1).

Gross income includes salary components counted for National Insurance purposes, pension
income, and rental income.

**Adjustments to the grade:**

- Two or more children enrolled in a ma'on or mishpachton: the income-test grade is reduced
  by one (a grade 7 family with two enrolled children is charged at grade 6).
- A grade-3 family with 2 enrolled children becomes grade 14; with 3 or more, grade 15.
- Miluim: if one parent served at least 60 days during 2024, the grade drops by one. This is
  a one-off Swords of Iron measure, valid from 01.09.2024, evidenced by form 3010.

**Grades set without an income test:**

- New immigrants (עולים חדשים) and returning residents (תושבים חוזרים): grade 3.
- Otef Aza residents: grade 3, through school year תשפ"ז inclusive.
- Lebanon-border towns adjacent to the fence (צמודי גדר): grade 3, for five years, תשפ"ו
  through תש"צ.
- Lebanon-border towns not adjacent to the fence: grade 5, for three years, תשפ"ו through
  תשפ"ח.

Where a family qualifies for different grades under different criteria, the grade giving the
**larger** subsidy is the one applied.

**Checking and correcting a grade.** The assigned grade can be looked up on the אגף לעידוד
תעסוקת הורים site or by calling *2969 / 1222-2969. Parents who believe the grade is wrong,
or whose circumstances changed mid-year, can ask the Ministry to correct it. There is a
simulator on the Ministry site for estimating a grade before applying.
