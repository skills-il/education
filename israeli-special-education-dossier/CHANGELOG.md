# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-03)

First full review since publication.

- Renewal is now counted in school years. A renewal must be referred by 31 March of the last
  school year the entitlement covers. The script used to print "decision date plus N years", which
  for a one-year characterisation decided in May landed after both the referral date and the
  15 May close.
- Added the wartime extension law (חוק חינוך מיוחד (הוראת שעה – חרבות ברזל) (הארכת זכאות),
  התשפ"ד-2023): decisions of 2020/21 to 2022/23 carry a fourth school year, with the
  stage-transition carve-out. The script applies it.
- Added that parents may change their setting choice until 25 June, the members-only renewal on
  parental consent, the document-age rules for mental disorders, the age range 3 to 21 and the
  31 December cut-off, and the objection-hearing postponement right.
- Choice matrix: suspected intellectual developmental disability carries a choice at every level.
- First Schedule: the autism, AD(H)D, physical and rare-disease rows now state the three-year
  paediatrician requirement and the closed list of psychologists for a first autism diagnosis.
- Objection clock: the circular counts from receipt of the protocol and the reasoned decision;
  the skill keeps the earlier date as the deadline. Team-stage filers are the student or a parent.
- Override procedure corrected: the parents' 14 days run from the committee receiving their
  choice, and the circular adds a second hearing. The Commission representation right is scoped
  to the override case, where the circular states it.
- Script: flags a statutory deadline on a Saturday (Interpretation Law s.10(c)), rejects invalid
  dates cleanly, and no longer prints the objections-committee ladder for a team decision.
- Removed the "legal advice" wording from the description opener in both languages, and added
  renewal to the triggers.

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-09)

Initial release.

Written against the post-2018 regime, because most public material still describes the system the
amendment replaced. Points where this skill deliberately departs from what circulates:

- The deciding body is ועדת זכאות ואפיון, and the appeal is an השגה to a ועדת השגה. The older
  ועדת ערר no longer exists.
- The appeal window is 21 days at both stages, not 30, and the filing window is hard: the statutory
  extension for special recorded reasons applies to the deciding body's time to ANSWER.
- There are four function levels, not three.
- Parents hold a right to choose the setting type, but not in every case. For several disabilities
  at the higher function levels there is no choice at all.
- Entitlement generally runs three years, except that EVERY suspected intellectual developmental
  disability characterisation, mild, moderate and severe or profound alike, is valid for one year.

Items deliberately NOT asserted because no primary source was found: any right to record a hearing,
any right for parents to address the committee members alone, and any appeal route against a
placement-team decision. These are recorded in the coverage checklist under "do not invent".
