# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-09)

Initial release.

Written against the post-2018 regime, because most public material still describes the system the
amendment replaced. Points where this skill deliberately departs from what circulates:

- The deciding body is ועדת זכאות ואפיון, and the appeal is an השגה to a ועדת השגה. The older
  ועדת ערר no longer exists.
- The appeal window is 21 days at both stages, not 30, and the filing window is hard: the statutory
  extension for special recorded reasons applies to the deciding body's time to ANSWER.
- There are four function levels, not three.
- Parents hold a right to choose the setting type, but not in every case. For several disabilities
  at the higher function levels there is no choice at all.
- Entitlement generally runs three years, except that EVERY suspected intellectual developmental
  disability characterisation, mild, moderate and severe or profound alike, is valid for one year.

Items deliberately NOT asserted because no primary source was found: any right to record a hearing,
any right for parents to address the committee members alone, and any appeal route against a
placement-team decision. These are recorded in the coverage checklist under "do not invent".
