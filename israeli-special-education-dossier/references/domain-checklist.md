# Domain Coverage Checklist, israeli-special-education-dossier

The coverage contract. A future update run reads this and flags any Must-cover item missing from
SKILL.md. The full research checklist, with every source URL and the verbatim statute quotations,
is held outside the published skill folder; citations live in evidence.json.

Checklist version: 1. Generated 2026-09-09.

## Must cover (core)

### Naming and the 2018 discontinuity
- [ ] That the deciding body is the entitlement and characterization committee, and that the older
      placement committee no longer decides entitlement. Why core: a parent reading an old page is
      sent to a body that will not hear them.
- [ ] That the appeal is an objection to an objections committee, and that the older appeals
      committee no longer exists under that name. Why core: same failure, on the step where a
      deadline is running.
- [ ] That since the amendment the parents hold the right to choose the setting type, subject to
      limits. Why core: most summaries still attribute that choice to the committee.

### Who decides and when
- [ ] The split between the school's multidisciplinary team and the committee, and that each has
      its own appeal. Why core: going to the wrong body costs a cycle.
- [ ] That a parent may refer the case directly and does not need the school's agreement.
      Why core: parents are routinely told the opposite.
- [ ] The annual calendar: the referral date, the date team discussions open, the two dates by
      which committee discussions conclude, and that the decision takes effect the following school
      year. Why core: a missed referral date costs a school year.
- [ ] The diagnosis-in-progress route and the continuation hearing. Why core: the most common
      legitimate way to stay inside the cycle.
- [ ] The off-cycle committee grounds, and that a new or updated diagnosis alone is not one.
      Why core: the most commonly attempted and refused request.

### Making the file decidable
- [ ] The admissible professional per disability, enumerated for every recognised category.
      Why core: bringing the wrong discipline's report is the single most common reason a file is
      not decided.
- [ ] Vision and hearing test requirements on first referral and for the relevant disabilities.
- [ ] The confidentiality waiver, and that separated or divorced parents both sign.

### Pre-hearing rights
- [ ] Advance disclosure of every document the committee may use, on the statutory notice period.
- [ ] The duty to invite both parents and student and to allow representation and submissions.
- [ ] The right to have a specialist doctor or a social worker invited on request.
- [ ] Delivery of the protocol, on its statutory timing.
- [ ] That no right to record the hearing was established, so the skill must not assert one.
      Why core: asserting a right that does not exist damages the parent's standing at the hearing.
- [ ] That at the committee stage the right is to have someone on your behalf, with counsel named
      expressly only at the appeal stage. Why core: precision here is the difference between a
      right and a hope.

### What is decided
- [ ] The four function levels, enumerated. Why core: sources saying three are describing something
      else, and the level drives the entitlement.
- [ ] The seven assessed domains.
- [ ] The choice matrix: which disability and function-level combinations carry a parental choice of
      setting type and which carry none. Why core: a flat "the parents choose" is wrong and will
      mislead exactly the families who have no choice.
- [ ] The three setting types where a choice exists, and that choosing a regular class delivers the
      entitlement as a personal basket.
- [ ] The quorum, its required members, and the chair's casting vote.

### After the decision
- [ ] How a parent gets NEW evidence in front of a decision-maker when the dispute turns on a new
      admissible opinion rather than on the existing record: send it to the objections-committee
      chair in advance and ask on the record that it be placed before the committee, ask the chair
      what the circular's objections-committee section permits, and keep the further-hearing route
      in view. Why core: filing an objection on exactly the record that produced the disputed level
      is the most common wasted appeal, and this is the operative question for a level dispute.
      The circular's objections-committee section expressly allows professional opinions and other
      current documents not presented at the entitlement committee, allows bringing them to the
      hearing without advance transmission, and recommends sending them to the chair beforehand.
- [ ] The deadline for the parents to notify their choice, and what happens if they miss it,
      including the priority to integration.
- [ ] The narrow override path, its three cumulative conditions, and that it is itself appealable.
- [ ] That a documents-only re-hearing carries no override power.
- [ ] That placement is a separate stage at the local authority which parents do not attend.
- [ ] That placement is frozen while an appeal is pending and the student stays put. Why core:
      parents believe the opposite and it changes whether they appeal at all.
- [ ] Both appeal deadlines and both decision deadlines, with the recorded-reasons extension.
- [ ] That the appeal decision being called final closes the administrative ladder only, that an
      administrative petition lies to the District Court, and that this is where a lawyer takes
      over. Why core: without it the skill dead-ends the family.
- [ ] The duration of entitlement, the renewal duty, and the earlier-review rules, INCLUDING that
      every suspected intellectual developmental disability characterisation, mild, moderate and
      severe or profound alike, is valid for one year only rather than three. Why core: a family
      told three years does not diarise a renewal that falls due two years early.
- [ ] Continuity of goals and supports across a transition between stages or institutions.

## Should cover
- [ ] The individual educational programme as the instrument that carries accommodations.
- [ ] The withholding of a document or part of a protocol, and its limits.
- [ ] Arrangements where parents are separated or divorced, including venue and separate hearings.
- [ ] The parents' membership of the multidisciplinary team when the service basket and the
      individual programme are set.
- [ ] That "in the presence of the members only" means TWO OPPOSITE things. It is a parent-side
      right to ask for at the multidisciplinary team, at the entitlement committee sitting as an
      appeal on a team decision, and at the objections committee. At the ORDINARY entitlement
      hearing it is the vote stage, taken without the parents unless the committee decides
      otherwise, and a member's request to exclude them must be granted. Why core: telling a parent
      to ask for it at the ordinary hearing asks them to request their own removal from the room.
- [ ] The right at the objections hearing to bring experts and other people on your behalf,
      expressly including a lawyer, and your own interpreter, and to ask the chair to summon others.
- [ ] The representation support available through the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with
      Disabilities.
- [ ] That the objections hearing cannot be held without the objecting party present.
- [ ] The referral and function-level questionnaires, and that a copy is delivered to the parents.

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)
- Which specific institution the child attends. Decided by the local authority's placement team,
  which the parents do not attend. Cover the handoff and the freeze rule only.
- Bagrut examination accommodations. A different circular and decision-maker.
- Bituach Leumi disabled child allowance, rehabilitative day care funding, and transport rates.
  Separate statutes and separate eligibility bodies.
- Pre-amendment committee procedure, except as a name mapping so an old letter can be read.
- Private diagnostic providers, their pricing and waiting lists. Commercial and non-statutory.
- Legal advice on a specific child's case, and drafting a court petition. Regulated-profession
  exposure. The skill produces preparation materials, not a pleading.

## Open items, do not invent these
- The phased-commencement order for the amendment. Its own wording was not read, so no clause
  should be quoted from it.
- Any right to record a hearing. None found.
- Per-disability assistance-hour criteria beyond the statutory minimums. The Second Schedule defers
  them to the circular and they were not extracted row by row.
- Any appeal route against a placement-team decision. Not established from a primary source.

- Year-specific wartime renewal guidance. Tell the parent to check the current year.
