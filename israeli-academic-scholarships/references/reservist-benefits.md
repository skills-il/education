# Reservist benefits for students (checked 23.11.2026)

Read this before quoting a reservist percentage, window or stacking result. Sources: the IDF Reserve Aid Fund page and 2026 regulations, the IDF students-in-reserve hub, and Kol Zchut for the personal grants.

## Tuition grant by academic year

| Year | Service test | Grant | Claim window |
|---|---|---|---|
| תשפ"ו | 50+ days, 23.10.2025 to 30.9.2026. Until 31.12.2025 only Order 8 days count, from 1.1.2026 all reserve days | % of university BA tuition by unit tier: A+ 100, A 85, B 65, C 30, D 30, E 22. Never above tuition paid | 1.8.2026 to 30.6.2027 (technological colleges from 1.11.2026) |
| תשפ"ה | 50+ days, 27.10.2024 to 30.9.2025 | Combat array 100% (up to NIS 11,653). Other units: see conflict below | **By 31.12.2026** |
| תשפ"ד | 60+ days, 7.10.2023 to 30.9.2024 | Combat array 100% (up to NIS 11,296). Other units: see conflict below | **By 31.12.2026** |
| תשפ"ז | No tuition-grant terms published as of 23.11.2026 | Do not project earlier terms | Not published |

**Which tier applies:** the tier in which most days were served. If days span tiers and the quota was not met in one, it is the tier holding at least 50% of the days. Days served in 2025 use the tier set for the unit in 2026.

**Official pages that disagree. State this, do not pick silently, and tell the student to file before the deadline so the Fund decides:**

- Other units in תשפ"ה and תשפ"ד: the Fund page says 33%. The tuition-grants page says 30% with caps of NIS 3,495 and NIS 3,388.
- תשפ"ה service: the Fund page says Order 8 only. The tuition-grants page also counts תע"מ after the order expired.
- תשפ"ד window: the web pages end it on 30.9.2024. The 2026 regulations print 30/9/2025.

**Also covered:** art institutions, completion studies, and institutions abroad recognized by the Ministry of Education's degree-evaluation unit. Reservists at MAHAT colleges funded by Haimash with 50+ days in tiers A+, A or B get an extra 10% automatically.

## Order and stacking

- A student who holds or may still apply for Mimadim, or is entitled to an MoD periphery scholarship, must use it first. The IDF page warns not to cancel Mimadim to apply for the Fund grant.
- If the government scholarship is below the student's grant ceiling, the Fund pays the difference up to that ceiling. Only the Fund decides whether a difference remains, including how it treats the 15% Mimadim withholds until the final year. Do not assume there is none: ask the Fund before the deadline and before giving up a claim.
- The Fund's page exempts excellence and volunteer scholarships from the double-funding bar. Its regulations also name institutional scholarships. **Declare every scholarship** and let the Fund rule.
- **Past years:** if a student never drew Mimadim for תשפ"ד or תשפ"ה, do not let the order rule stall the claim. Ask the MoD (*5266) whether Mimadim or a periphery scholarship can still pay that year, and **file with the Fund (WhatsApp 052-3964584, Sunday to Thursday 08:00-15:00) by 31.12.2026 without waiting for that answer**.
- **Not entitled to Mimadim or a periphery scholarship:** go to the Fund. The Mimadim window limits only the FIRST application, so a current recipient stays on Mimadim.
- Personal and family grants are not tuition scholarships and are unaffected.
- MoD periphery: combat reservists entitled to full funding are excluded from tracks 44, 45 and 46. Combat-support reservists who received 30% are topped up to 100% on 44 and 45, and to 50% on 46. For other tiers, ask *5266.

## Private-lesson reimbursement (against receipts)

The Fund requires a receipt or tax invoice, not a handwritten confirmation.

- **תשפ"ד and תשפ"ה:** Order 8 only. Combat array with 100+ days up to NIS 3,000, anyone with 21+ days up to NIS 2,000. **Claim by 31.12.2026.**
- **תשפ"ו:** 21+ days overlapping the year: up to NIS 3,000 a calendar year (A+, A), 2,500 (B), 2,000 (C to E).
- **Claim limit:** every Fund entitlement from 7.10.2023 to 31.12.2025, including lessons in the first months of תשפ"ו, by 31.12.2026. Other entitlements that began on or after 1.1.2026, with no specific published window: within one year of the event, so 2026 claims can start expiring from 1.1.2027. File receipts now.

## PEREACH supplement

NIS 2,600 for PEREACH tutors with 10+ active days, July to July, plus NIS 900 for an active reservist (NIS 3,500 total). Automatic.

## Credit-point relief (through the institution's reserve coordinator)

- **תשפ"ד**, by total days: 30-60 days 6 credits, 61-99 days 8, 100+ days 10. Medicine, health professions, engineering and licensed professions except teaching get 8 at 100+ days.
- **From תשפ"ה** (continued in תשפ"ו and תשפ"ז): 6 credits for 21+ days in a semester, 35+ days in the year, 60+ days that began in the 3 months before the year, or a parent of children up to 13 whose spouse meets one of these (from תשפ"ו the spouse must serve in a frontline role). 8 credits for 35+ days in a semester, or a parent of children up to 13 whose spouse is in this group.
- MA: up to 4 credits. BA cap: 10 credits across the whole degree, including earlier relief.
