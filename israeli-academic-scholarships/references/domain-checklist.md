# Domain Coverage Checklist — israeli-academic-scholarships

Generated: 2026-04-27 via research on: education.gov.il, che.org.il, students.gov.il, perach.org.il, hachvana.mod.gov.il, fidfimpact.org, nbn.org.il, iseffoundation.org, milgapo.co.il, adams.academy.ac.il, yadhanadiv.org.il, womeninacademy.huji.ac.il, weizmann.ac.il, kolzchut.org.il, knesset.gov.il, nevo.co.il

This file is the contract the skill is judged against. Every "Must cover" item must be addressed in SKILL.md or supporting files. Future `update-skill` runs re-read this file and flag any item still missing.

## Must cover (core)

- [x] **PEREACH (פר"ח) tutoring grant** — Tutoring-for-grant scheme run from the Weizmann Institute. ~114 hours (100 mentoring + 14 training) yields NIS 7,000; 140-hour track yields NIS 10,000 (some municipalities offer enhanced NIS 10,000 via Mifal HaPais). Open to undergraduates at MALAG-recognized institutions. — source: https://www.perach.org.il/ — why core: largest-volume scholarship in Israel, applicable to nearly any student regardless of background; first thing a typical sample profile should be matched against.

- [x] **Atid combat-veteran scholarship + IMPACT! (FIDF) + Mimadim LiLimudim** — Atid Foundation runs combat-veteran tracks; IMPACT! grants USD 5,000/year per fellow, funded by individual donors; Mimadim LiLimudim (Ministry of Defense, hachvana.mod.gov.il) covers full tuition for the combat-veteran cohort + special populations, ~3,230 fellows in תשפ"ו (academic year 2025-2026). — source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/MainEducation/HachvanaScholarship/Pages/UniformToStudies.aspx + https://fidfimpact.org/ — why core: every veteran user with a lochem profile is in the eligibility set; biggest single bucket of military-linked scholarships.

- [x] **Pikadon Hayashi (personal deposit) for tuition** — Discharged-soldier and Sherut Leumi alumni's personal deposit can pay tuition only at MALAG-recognized institutions or institutions recognized by the Ministry of Economy / Education / Tourism / Health / Public Security; usable within 5 years of release for studies, psychometric prep, mechina, ישיבה גבוהה, vocational training. — source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/GrantAndDeposit/DepositUpTo5/Pages/education.aspx — why core: not technically a scholarship but is the universal first-source-of-funds for any post-service student; must be surfaced before recommending external scholarships.

- [x] **Periphery scholarship — Ministry of Defense (אזורי עדיפות לאומית)** — Covers up to 100% of first-year BA tuition (max ~NIS 11,653 in 2025) for discharged soldiers / Sherut Leumi alumni who lived in a national-priority-area yishuv 5 of the 6 years before studies. Cannot be combined with other periphery scholarships. — source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/MainEducation/HachvanaScholarship/Pages/Perypheria45.aspx — why core: the canonical "periphery resident" scholarship; the user filter "periphery resident" maps directly to this rule set, and the exclusivity rule is irreversible per cycle.

- [x] **Nefesh B'Nefesh / Student Authority (Minhal HaStudentim) for olim** — Oleh Chadash students under 27 (BA) eligible for partial/full tuition via the Student Authority (Ministry of Aliyah & Integration). Repatriate students enrolling within 3 years of aliyah may receive up to ~90% tuition discount via "Masa Harhava." NBN coordinates additional foundation scholarships for olim from North America/UK. — source: https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/government-services/rights-and-benefits/student-authority-tuition-benefits/ + https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/education/higher-education/scholarships-and-loans-for-university-and-college-students/ — why core: every oleh chadash filter must surface this; it is the dominant path, not Mahar/Achi (see Should).

- [x] **Sherut Leumi-Ezrachi post-service rights** — Sherut Leumi alumni receive a Pikadon, are eligible for the periphery scholarship (above), and can use the deposit for studies up to 5 years post-service; rights flow through the Ministry of Defense hachvana portal in parallel with discharged-soldier rights. — source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/luemi11/Pages/default.aspx + https://aminadav.org.il/ — why core: dedicated track in scope; treated parallel to discharged-soldier rights but with separate eligibility checks.

- [x] **Ministry of Education socioeconomic scholarship (current branding: MilGo via Milgapo)** — BA / hand-engineer / mechina students at MALAG- or MAHAT-recognized institutions; awarded by socioeconomic score (income, household composition, residence in a priority area, military service). תשפ"ו cycle opened 14.12.2025; awards range NIS 5,000–12,480, and the current track is given without a volunteer-hour requirement. Replaced/sits alongside the historical SHEFI socioeconomic scholarship. — source: https://milgapo.co.il/?milga=_edu.gov + https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/MadaTech/MadeayHatechnologia/2025/scholarships2025.pdf — why core: highest-volume needs-based government scholarship for BA students.

- [x] **Rashi Foundation — Katzir + Rakia tracks** — Privately-funded foundation co-founded by Edmond J. Safra et al. NIS 5,000–18,000/year, multi-year (3-4 years), periphery focus. Katzir track for university/college students; Rakia track for additional periphery/excellence. Single-mother track ~NIS 16,000. — source: https://milgapo.co.il/?milga=_rashi-katzir + https://milgapo.co.il/?milga=_rashi-rakia — why core: largest privately-funded scholarship in Israel by reach; the canonical foundation grant for periphery / socioeconomic-disadvantaged users.

- [x] **ISEF Foundation (Israel Scholarship Education Foundation)** — Active in 2026 as a separate entity (NOT merged). ~450 BA-to-PhD scholarships in Israel + ~20 international MD/PhD/postdoc fellowships. Targets underserved-background students with strong academic merit; multi-year mentorship + leadership program in addition to financial grant. — source: https://www.iseffoundation.org/ + https://www.isef.org.il/ — why core: distinct active entity, routinely matched to periphery + first-generation + Mizrahi/Sephardi students; user-profile filter for "low-income with strong academics" maps here.

- [x] **University excellence scholarships for incoming freshmen** — Pattern across all major institutions: HUJI, TAU (up to NIS 5,000 one-time gift), BIU, BGU, Technion, etc. award merit scholarships at admission based on bagrut + psychometric scores. תשפ"ו tuition baseline: NIS 12,017 (set by MALAG). — source: https://go.tau.ac.il/he/ba/milgot + https://info.huji.ac.il/registration-process/prizes — why core: every incoming freshman should be checked against their target institution's automatic merit scholarship; pattern, not exhaustive list.

- [x] **Adams Fellowship (Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities)** — Doctoral fellowship for 2nd-year PhDs across all fields. Full tuition exemption + NIS 100,000/year stipend + USD 3,000/year travel grant; up to 4 years. Application is via institution rector. The 2026-27 cycle opened 26.10.2025 and closed 5.1.2026. — source: https://adams.academy.ac.il/fellowships/ + https://www.academy.ac.il/RichText/GeneralPage.aspx?nodeId=847 — why core: most prestigious Israeli doctoral fellowship; canonical match for top PhD candidates.

- [x] **Rothschild Fellowship (Yad Hanadiv)** — Postdoctoral fellowships for Israeli permanent residents who received their PhD from an Israeli university after a rolling cutoff (currently November 2021 for the 2025 cycle); fellowship abroad starting 1 June – 1 December 2025; supplementary funds capped at USD 75,000/year. — source: https://www.yadhanadiv.org.il/rothschild-fellows/ — why core: canonical Israeli postdoctoral-abroad fellowship; required for any "postdoc abroad" filter even though it sits at the tail end of graduate-level scope.

- [x] **PBC / MALAG Scholarships for Outstanding Doctoral Students from Periphery + Maof program** — The Council for Higher Education (המל"ג) operates dedicated tracks: outstanding-doctoral-students-from-periphery scholarships, plus Maof scholarships for outstanding young scientists from the Arab community to be hired by PBC-funded institutions. — source: https://che.org.il/en/scholarships-grants-students-faculty/pbc-scholarship-program-for-outstanding-doctoral-students/ + https://che.org.il/en/scholarships-integration-outstanding-faculty/ — why core: MALAG is the named authoritative source; covers two distinct user filters (periphery PhDs, Arab-Israeli faculty pipeline).

- [x] **Application timeline awareness (Israeli academic year cycle)** — Most scholarship deadlines cluster May–September each year for the upcoming October-start academic year; a few open earlier (Adams Fellowship window October–January for the *following* academic year, Mimadim LiLimudim windows tied to discharge cycles, MilGo opens December). The skill must surface deadlines first when ranking matches, not last. — source: https://www.gov.il/en/service/apply-online-for-a-scholarship-from-the-student-authority + aggregate of all sources above — why core: a scholarship match is useless if the deadline already passed; deadline-aware ranking is the core differentiating logic of this skill.

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)

- [x] **Bagrut excellence track for incoming freshmen** — High-bagrut-average + high-psychometric students often receive automatic merit scholarships at registration time without needing to apply separately (HUJI, TAU, Technion). Surface this as a passive-eligibility check based on user-supplied scores. — source: https://info.huji.ac.il/registration-process/prizes — why advanced: pattern-based, varies per institution; not always self-reported by users.

- [x] **NBN-coordinated olim foundation grants (Mahar / Achi context)** — Mahar (career advancement for olim) and Achi (English-speaking olim integration) appear in older NBN literature, but per the current 2026 NBN higher-education page the listed channels for olim are Student Authority (Minhal HaStudentim), ISEF, Limudim B'Yisrael, foundations, and per-university Misrad HaKlita counselors. Users hearing "Mahar" or "Achi" should be redirected to the active NBN higher-education portal rather than treating them as standalone scholarship products. — source: https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/education/higher-education/scholarships-and-loans-for-university-and-college-students/ — why advanced: name recognition is high among olim, but the actual canonical paths are the broader NBN/Student Authority channels — must hedge claims.

- [x] **Masa post-program scholarships** — Masa Israel alumni (post-Masa) can be evaluated for follow-on academic scholarships such as Sofaer Global MBA at Tel Aviv University. Eligibility is restricted (no prior Israeli academic stay >4 months, no Israeli citizenship-by-residence, age 18–35). — source: https://www.masaisrael.org/funding/grants-scholarships/ + https://international.tau.ac.il/masa_scholarships — why advanced: only relevant for diaspora-origin users who came via Masa and are continuing to academic studies; narrow but well-defined.

- [x] **Women in STEM scholarships** — Hebrew University offers free first-year tuition to female students entering CS or Math degrees. The Weizmann Institute promotes-women-in-science unit aggregates national doctoral + postdoctoral-abroad fellowships (10 doctoral seats per year). HUJI also runs an aggregator at womeninacademy.huji.ac.il. — source: https://womeninacademy.huji.ac.il/ + https://www.weizmann.ac.il/women-in-science/resources/awards-scholarships/external-scholarships — why advanced: high-impact for the targeted user filter ("women in STEM"), but coverage is institution-by-institution rather than a single national scheme.

- [x] **Athlete excellence scholarships** — Mifal HaPais runs a national track for athletes on the Israeli national representative team. Universities (BGU, OpenU, etc.) award internal scholarships to athletes representing university teams. Many municipalities (Nes Tziona ~NIS 2,000, Kadima-Tzoran, Be'er Tuvia, Herzliya) run small periodic local funds. — source: https://www.pais.co.il/info/collaborations.aspx + https://in.bgu.ac.il/welcome/Pages/Scholarship-athletes.aspx — why advanced: small targeted population; needs national-team verification; the municipal layer is hyper-local and noisy.

- [x] **Reserve duty (miluim) addition to PEREACH** — Tosefet l'milgat PEREACH for students who served reserve duty during the academic year; stacks on top of the base PEREACH grant. — source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%AA_%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%92%D7%AA_%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%97_%D7%9C%D7%A1%D7%98%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A0%D7%98%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%91%D7%92%D7%99%D7%9F_%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9D — why advanced: applies only to PEREACH recipients who also served miluim during the academic year; specific stacking case worth surfacing for relevant profiles.

- [x] **Faculty-specific awards (medicine, engineering, humanities, exact sciences)** — Per-faculty internal awards exist at all major institutions (e.g., TAU School of Physics in-school positions and graduate scholarships, HUJI Brain Sciences via Edmond J. Safra Center, Technion Russell Berrie Nanotechnology fellowship). Surface as a pattern: tell the user to check their target faculty's own scholarships page after applying for the broader institutional awards. — source: https://exact-sciences.tau.ac.il/physics-h/school_positions_scholarships + https://elsc.huji.ac.il/he/ — why advanced: too dispersed for an exhaustive list; pattern-level coverage is enough.

- [x] **Russell Berrie Nanotechnology Institute (Technion) — Norman Seiden Fellowship** — Targeted postdoctoral fellowship to retain top Israeli talent in nanotechnology and optoelectronics. — source: https://rbni.technion.ac.il/about — why advanced: highly specific to one faculty; example of the faculty-specific pattern above.

- [x] **Alix De Rothschild Fellowship (humanities-focused, via Israel Academy of Sciences)** — Distinct from the Yad Hanadiv Rothschild Fellowship; postdoctoral, humanities-leaning track via the Academy. — source: https://www.academy.ac.il/RichText/GeneralPage.aspx?nodeId=1696 — why advanced: name collision with the main Rothschild Fellowship causes user confusion; needs to be disambiguated, not over-promoted.

- [x] **Single-discharged-soldier law statutory framework** — חוק חיילים משוחררים (קליטת חיילים משוחררים), תשנ"ד-1994 codifies the Pikadon and Mimadim entitlements; the Council for Higher Education Law (תשי"ח-1958) governs MALAG scholarship authority. Reference for explaining *why* a particular scholarship exists or is bounded. — source: https://www.nevo.co.il/ + https://main.knesset.gov.il/ — why advanced: legal grounding to justify rule statements; not user-facing in normal flow but useful for source-of-truth disputes.

- [x] **Mifal HaPais social-good scholarship partnerships** — National lottery operator that co-funds many of the above (PEREACH enhanced track, athlete scholarships, regional periphery scholarships). Often invisible to users but is the funding source. — source: https://www.pais.co.il/info/collaborations.aspx — why advanced: the user doesn't apply to Mifal HaPais directly in most cases; surface it only when the user asks "who funds this."

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)

- **High-school / K-12 student scholarships (mlgot tichon, mlgot tichon mtsuyan)** — out of scope per skill definition (higher-education only) — related skill: would belong under a future `israeli-k12-scholarships` skill, not this one.

- **Bituach Leumi cost-of-living grants for students (havtachat hachnasa, mlgat kium)** — these are general welfare benefits, not academic scholarships, and are means-tested at a different threshold — related skill: `israeli-bituach-leumi` handles them.

- **Tuition financing / student loans (halvaa l'sachar limud, halvaot Bank Mizrahi-Tefahot, halvaot Bank Hapoalim, halvaot Otzma)** — financing instruments, not grants; user pays back with interest — related skill: a future `israeli-student-loans` would handle tuition financing as a separate concern.

- **MFA scholarships for foreign (non-Israeli) students** — gov.il/en/service/scholarships_application_for_academic_studies_in_israel is exclusively for inbound international students; Israeli citizens and olim follow different paths (above) — related skill: out of scope; covered by inbound-study marketing channels, not this skill.

- **Yeshiva / kollel stipends (mlgot kollel, kitzva mishpachti l'avrechim)** — religious-study stipends paid by Ministry of Religious Services and yeshiva networks; not academic-degree-tied — related skill: a future `israeli-yeshiva-rights` would handle them.

- **Vocational and Technological Training Fund grants (Mahat, mosdot mukhrim l'pi mahat)** — handasai certification and vocational tracks have their own funding pipeline (Pikadon usable, plus separate vocational grants) — related skill: borderline; if `israeli-handasai-certification` exists or is added, route there. Cross-reference but don't enumerate.

- **Disability-specific scholarships (mlgot l'studentim im moglut, Tza'adim Ktanim, Lev LaLomed)** — disability-targeted scholarships are a distinct domain with their own intake process via Misrad HaRevacha and dedicated foundations — related skill: future `israeli-students-with-disabilities` handles it.

- **Holocaust survivor descendant scholarships (Claims Conference, Keren la-Refuah)** — distinct eligibility (descendant of survivor) and distinct funding pipeline — related skill: out of scope; specialty channel.

- **MK Israel Foundation** — could not verify as an active named scholarship body in 2026 research; ISMK ("Israel & Sephardi Mizrahi Kollel" community grants) appears to be a different organization; do NOT include in skill content without further verification.

- **SHEFI (שפ"י) as a standalone scholarship brand** — SHEFI is the Ministry of Education's Psychological Counseling Service (שירות פסיכולוגי ייעוצי), NOT a scholarship program. The Ministry of Education socioeconomic scholarship is a separate product (now branded "MilGo" via the Milgapo platform). The skill must NOT conflate the two.
