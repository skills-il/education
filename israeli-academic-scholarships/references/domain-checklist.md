# Domain Coverage Checklist — israeli-academic-scholarships

Generated: 2026-04-27 via research on: education.gov.il, che.org.il, students.gov.il, pereach.org.il, hachvana.mod.gov.il, nbn.org.il, isef.org, milgapo.co.il, adams.academy.ac.il, yadhanadiv.org.il, knesset.gov.il, nevo.co.il

## Must cover (core)

- [x] **PEREACH (פר"ח) tutoring grant** — Largest-volume scholarship in Israel, applicable to nearly any undergraduate student regardless of background. Tutoring-for-grant scheme. — source: https://www.perach.org.il/ — why core: first thing a typical sample profile should be matched against.
- [x] **Atid combat-veteran scholarship + Mimadim LiLimudim (MoD)** — Atid Foundation runs combat-veteran tracks; Ministry of Defense Mimadim LiLimudim covers full tuition for combat veterans + special populations. — source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/MainEducation/HachvanaScholarship/Pages/UniformToStudies.aspx — why core: every veteran user with a lochem profile.
- [x] **Pikadon Hayashi (personal deposit) for tuition** — Discharged-soldier and Sherut Leumi alumni's personal deposit can pay tuition only at MALAG-recognized institutions; usable within 5 years of release. — source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/GrantAndDeposit/DepositUpTo5/Pages/education.aspx — why core: not technically a scholarship but is the universal first-source-of-funds for any post-service student.
- [x] **Periphery scholarship — MoD (אזורי עדיפות לאומית)** — Up to 100% of first-year BA tuition for discharged soldiers / Sherut Leumi alumni who lived in a national-priority-area yishuv 5 of 6 years before studies. — source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/MainEducation/HachvanaScholarship/Pages/Perypheria45.aspx — why core: canonical "periphery resident" scholarship.
- [x] **Nefesh B'Nefesh / Student Authority (Minhal HaStudentim) for olim** — Oleh Chadash students under 27 (BA) eligible for partial/full tuition via Student Authority (Ministry of Aliyah & Integration). Repatriate students enrolling within 3 years of aliyah may receive up to ~90% tuition discount. — source: https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/government-services/rights-and-benefits/student-authority-tuition-benefits/ — why core: dominant path for olim, not Mahar/Achi.
- [x] **Sherut Leumi-Ezrachi post-service rights** — Sherut Leumi alumni receive a Pikadon, are eligible for periphery scholarship, and can use deposit for studies up to 5 years post-service. — source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/luemi11/Pages/default.aspx — why core: parallel to discharged-soldier rights but with separate eligibility.
- [x] **Ministry of Education socioeconomic scholarship (current branding: MilGo via Milgapo)** — BA / hand-engineer / mechina students at MALAG- or MAHAT-recognized institutions; awarded by socioeconomic score. תשפ"ו awards range NIS 5,000–12,480. — source: https://milgapo.co.il/ — why core: highest-volume needs-based government scholarship.
- [x] **Rashi Foundation — Katzir + Rakia tracks** — Privately-funded, NIS 5,000–18,000/year, multi-year, periphery focus. — source: https://milgapo.co.il/?milga=_rashi-katzir — why core: largest privately-funded scholarship in Israel by reach.
- [x] **ISEF Foundation (Israel Scholarship Education Foundation)** — Active in 2026 as a separate entity. ~450 BA-to-PhD scholarships in Israel + ~20 international MD/PhD/postdoc fellowships. Targets underserved-background students with strong academic merit. — source: https://www.iseffoundation.org/ — why core: distinct active entity, routinely matched to first-generation + periphery students.
- [x] **University excellence scholarships for incoming freshmen** — Pattern across HUJI, TAU, BIU, BGU, Technion, etc. — merit scholarships at admission based on bagrut + psychometric scores. — source: https://info.huji.ac.il/registration-process/prizes — why core: every incoming freshman should be checked against their target institution's automatic merit scholarship.
- [x] **Adams Fellowship (Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities)** — Doctoral fellowship for 2nd-year PhDs; full tuition exemption + stipend + travel grant; up to 4 years. — source: https://adams.academy.ac.il/fellowships/ — why core: most prestigious Israeli doctoral fellowship.
- [x] **Rothschild Fellowship (Yad Hanadiv)** — Postdoctoral fellowships for Israeli PhDs going abroad. — source: https://www.yadhanadiv.org.il/rothschild-fellows/ — why core: canonical Israeli postdoctoral-abroad fellowship.
- [x] **PBC / MALAG Scholarships for Outstanding Doctoral Students from Periphery + Maof program** — Council for Higher Education tracks. — source: https://che.org.il/en/scholarships-grants-students-faculty/pbc-scholarship-program-for-outstanding-doctoral-students/ — why core: covers two distinct user filters (periphery PhDs, Arab-Israeli faculty pipeline).
- [x] **Application timeline awareness (Israeli academic year cycle)** — Most scholarship deadlines cluster May–September each year for the upcoming October-start academic year; some open earlier. — why core: a scholarship match is useless if the deadline already passed; deadline-aware ranking is the core differentiating logic.

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)

- [x] **Bagrut excellence track for incoming freshmen** — High-bagrut + high-psychometric students often receive automatic merit scholarships at registration time without a separate application. — why advanced: pattern-based, varies per institution.
- [x] **Olim aliases (Mahar / Achi) routing** — Users hearing "Mahar" or "Achi" should be redirected to the active NBN higher-education portal rather than treating them as standalone scholarship products. — why advanced: name recognition is high among olim, but actual canonical paths are Student Authority / ISEF / foundations.
- [x] **Masa post-program scholarships** — Masa Israel alumni can be evaluated for follow-on academic scholarships (e.g., Sofaer Global MBA at TAU). Eligibility narrow (no prior Israeli academic stay >4 months, no Israeli citizenship-by-residence, age 18–35). — why advanced: only relevant for diaspora-origin users who came via Masa.
- [x] **Women in STEM scholarships** — Hebrew University offers free first-year tuition to female CS/Math students; Weizmann promotes-women-in-science unit aggregates national doctoral + postdoctoral-abroad fellowships. — why advanced: high-impact for the targeted user filter, but coverage is institution-by-institution rather than national.
- [x] **Athlete excellence scholarships** — Mifal HaPais runs a national track for athletes on the Israeli national representative team. Universities (BGU, OpenU) award internal scholarships to athletes representing university teams. — why advanced: small targeted population; needs national-team verification.
- [x] **Reserve duty (miluim) addition to PEREACH** — Tosefet l'milgat PEREACH for students who served reserve duty during the academic year. — why advanced: applies only to PEREACH recipients who also served miluim.
- [x] **Faculty-specific awards pattern** — Per-faculty internal awards exist at all major institutions. Surface as a pattern: tell the user to check their target faculty's own scholarships page. — why advanced: too dispersed for an exhaustive list.
- [x] **Single-discharged-soldier law statutory framework** — חוק חיילים משוחררים (קליטת חיילים משוחררים), תשנ"ד-1994 codifies the Pikadon and Mimadim entitlements; the Council for Higher Education Law (תשי"ח-1958) governs MALAG scholarship authority. — why advanced: legal grounding to justify rule statements.

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)

- **High-school / K-12 student scholarships** — out of scope per skill definition (higher-education only) — related skill: would belong under a future K-12 skill.
- **Bituach Leumi cost-of-living grants for students (havtachat hachnasa)** — these are general welfare benefits, not academic scholarships — related skill: `israeli-bituach-leumi` handles welfare benefits.
- **Tuition financing / student loans** — financing instruments, not grants; user pays back with interest — related skill: out of scope; a future student-loans skill would handle them.
- **MFA scholarships for foreign (non-Israeli) students** — exclusively for inbound international students — related skill: out of scope.
- **Yeshiva / kollel stipends** — religious-study stipends, not academic-degree-tied — related skill: out of scope.
- **Disability-specific scholarships** — distinct domain with its own intake process via Misrad HaRevacha — related skill: future disability-specific skill.
- **Holocaust survivor descendant scholarships** — distinct eligibility and funding pipeline — related skill: out of scope.
- **MK Israel Foundation** — could not verify as an active named scholarship body in 2026 research; do NOT include without further verification.
- **SHEFI as a standalone scholarship brand** — SHEFI is the Ministry of Education's Psychological Counseling Service, NOT a scholarship program. The Ministry of Education socioeconomic scholarship is now branded "MilGo" via Milgapo. The skill must NOT conflate these.
