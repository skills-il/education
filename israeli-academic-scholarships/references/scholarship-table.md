# Israeli Scholarship Quick-Reference Table

A consolidated lookup for the major Israeli higher-education scholarships referenced by this skill. Use as a fast filter before deep-diving into any specific track. All amounts are illustrative and refresh annually — always verify on the source URL listed in evidence.json.

## Universal-eligibility (most students should check)

| Scholarship | Profile filter | Typical grant | Application timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| PEREACH (פר"ח) | Any undergraduate | NIS 7,000 (~114 hrs) or NIS 10,000 (140 hrs) | Annual cycle, opens ahead of academic year |
| MilGo (MoE socioeconomic) | BA / hand-engineer / mechina at MALAG/MAHAT institution | NIS 5,000–12,480 | Cycle opens December; check Milgapo |
| University merit at admission | Incoming freshman with strong bagrut + psychometric | One-time grant per institution; HUJI/TAU/Technion vary | Automatic at registration |

## Post-service (IDF + Sherut Leumi)

| Scholarship | Profile filter | Typical grant | Application timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pikadon Hayashi | Discharged soldier or Sherut Leumi alum, within 5-year window | The user's own deposit | Anytime within 5-year window |
| MoD periphery scholarship | Lived in priority-area yishuv 5 of 6 years before studies | Up to 100% first-year BA tuition (~NIS 11,653) | Annual; cannot stack with other periphery |
| Mimadim LiLimudim | Combat veterans + special populations | Full tuition; ~3,230 fellows/year | Annual; tied to discharge cycle |
| Atid Foundation | Combat veterans (foundation track) | Per-track grant | Check current cycle |
