# Israeli Academic Scholarships

## Legal notice

This skill provides general information about published scholarship programmes.
It is not a determination of your eligibility and it is not an application that
has been submitted. Every scholarship listed here is awarded by its own body
(the Ministry of Defense, the Ministry of Education, a university, or a private
foundation) under criteria that body sets and can change, and only that body
decides who receives a grant. Amounts, tiers, caps and dates are those published
for the cycle stated beside them, and cycles reopen with different terms. Nothing
here is a promise of funding, and no output of this skill should be relied on in
place of the official programme page or the institution's scholarship office.
Verify eligibility, the current cycle's amounts and every deadline against the
official source before you apply or decline to apply. This is a free information
tool operated by an AI model, without the involvement, review or approval of the
awarding body, a lawyer or any other licensed professional. It does not examine
your documents or verify your details with the awarding body. It may err, omit
data or reach a wrong conclusion.
It is not a substitute for advice that takes into account each person's
particular data and needs, and any use of it is the user's sole responsibility.

## Problem

Israel's scholarships are fragmented across the Ministry of Defense, the IDF Reserve Aid Fund, the Council for Higher Education, the Student Authority, foundations and universities. Since October 2023 separate reservist grants add their own yearly windows and stacking rules, and several tracks close with no second chance for that year.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Profile the user

Before recommending scholarships, gather the dimensions that determine eligibility. Don't ask one at a time, ask in a single batched intake.

| Dimension | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Degree level (BA, MA, PhD, postdoc) | Different scholarships per level; doctoral fellowships are an entirely different category |
| Institution (university name, faculty) | MALAG-recognized vs MAHAT-recognized vs other; each university has its own merit scholarships |
| Year of study | Some scholarships only target incoming freshmen, others target second-year-and-above |
| Field of study (humanities/social sciences vs exact/life sciences/math) | Adams Fellowship excludes humanities |
| Military service status (discharged soldier, Sherut Leumi, exemption) | Unlocks Pikadon, Mimadim LiLimudim, MoD periphery scholarship |
| Discharge certificate and service type | Gold discharge opens Mimadim. Special populations qualify with an honorable discharge and no combat role. Reservist grants depend on the unit's activity tier |
| Reserve days per period since 7.10.2023, the unit's activity tier (מדרג), and any child under 14 | Each grant counts a different window, so one cumulative total is not enough. The tier sets the tuition-grant percentage |
| Aliyah status (oleh, child of olim, citizen-oleh, returning minor) and date of status | Student Authority / Minhal HaStudentim |
| Residence history (priority-area yishuv 5 of 6 years before studies) and any funded mechina | MoD periphery tracks 45 and 46 |
| Evacuated household status (פינוי 7.10.2023 from עוטף עזה / north) | Unlocks evacuee tracks (Heznek La'Atid, university emergency funds) |
| Bereaved-family / wounded-soldier status (יתום מערכות ישראל / נכה צה"ל) | Family & Commemoration or Rehabilitation Branch, plus the discharged-soldiers division |
| Bagrut average + psychometric score | University merit scholarships, Adams |
| Household socioeconomic status | MilGo, Rashi, ISEF. MilGo levels are set relative to each year's applicants. ISEF weighs background alongside academics and publishes no bagrut cutoff. |
| Athletic status (national-team representative) | Mifal HaPais athlete track |

### Step 2: Check the widely applicable tracks first

These three are worth checking for almost every Israeli student:

| Scholarship | Universal? | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| PEREACH (פר"ח) | Worth checking for most students, including graduate students | Base grant NIS 7,000 for about 114 hours (100 mentoring + 14 training). An enhanced NIS 10,000 track for 130 hours runs with Mifal HaPais in some cities only, and not for students holding another Mifal HaPais scholarship. תשפ"ז registration opened in early September and ends around the end of November, first-come-first-served. |
| MilGo (run by CHE and PEREACH) | First-degree, mechina, hand-engineer or technician students with no prior degree | BA / mechina levels: NIS 12,480 / 10,000 / 7,500 / 5,000. Hand-engineer / technician: NIS 8,600 / 6,000 / 4,500 / 3,000. Set relative to that year's applicants. Excluded, among others: other aid above NIS 8,400 (state-funded institutions, with listed exceptions such as PEREACH) or NIS 16,800 (others). Full list in `references/scholarship-table.md`. |
| University merit scholarship at admission | Worth checking for any incoming freshman | Bagrut + psychometric thresholds vary per institution; many are automatic at registration. The university tuition figure is NIS 12,203 for תשפ"ז (NIS 12,017 for תשפ"ו). Actual tuition varies by program, so use the student's own figure. |

### Step 3: Run the post-service checks (if applicable)

If the user served in the IDF or Sherut Leumi:

| Track | Eligibility | What it pays |
|---|---|---|
| Pikadon Hayashi | Any discharged soldier or Sherut Leumi alum, within the post-discharge window (5 years for most; after that the MoD has separate deposit rules, so check hachvana.mod.gov.il) | Personal deposit (the user's own money), usable for tuition at MALAG-recognized institutions and for psychometric prep, mechina, vocational training, or ישיבה גבוהה |
| MoD periphery scholarship (אזורי עדיפות לאומית) | Track 45: registered address in a national-priority area 5 of the 6 years before studies, AND within the post-discharge window: 5 years standard, 10 years for active reservists and lone soldiers | **Only at institutions whose budget the state participates in.** Up to 100% of first-year BA tuition, capped at NIS 12,017 (תשפ"ו), paid into the Pikadon account. Only one of MoD periphery tracks 44, 45, 46. Track 44 (studying at a priority-area institution) needs a signed commitment and a completed first year; it funds the first year in full after full service, and up to 50% after 12-month national service. Track 46 (funded mechina begun by the fourth year after discharge) pays up to 50% for up to three years, NIS 6,008.5 a year in תשפ"ו, **must be applied for again every year**, and **cannot combine with Mimadim**. |
| Mimadim LiLimudim | Gold honorable-discharge combat veterans, plus honorable-discharge special populations: parents' Tashmash, family-payments recipients, lone soldiers, Arab, Druze and Circassian veterans, and new immigrants. First-application window: 5 years post-discharge (regular service), extended by 1 year (to 6) for bagrut completion / mechina / psychometric within those 5 years, by 2 years (to 7) for continuous career service of a year or more, and by 3 years (to 8) for both. תשפ"ז is open 8.11.2026 to 31.8.2027. | Up to 100% of the university tuition ceiling, paid in stages, for 3 study years, or 4 for engineering, medicine or architecture. Each year but the last refunds up to 85% of tuition actually paid: NIS 10,372.55 on the NIS 12,203 ceiling in תשפ"ז. The final year tops up the withheld 15% for prior years, against a study approval and payment statement (not receipts) for EVERY funded year, so keep them. It is a refund: the student pays first. Tuition above the ceiling is not covered. The page lists BA, MA and art-certificate studies (MA students should confirm at *5266), not hand-engineer or technician studies, which have separate MoD funding of 90%. No volunteering hours. |
| Mashpim BeLimudim (MoD + Rothschild Foundation) | Discharged combat and combat-support soldiers from defined special populations, studying in תשפ"ז | NIS 12,000 living stipend for 90 social-activity hours + 10 training hours. Extra NIS 6,000 for 60+ reserve days in 2027 in a frontline-designated unit. Register 3.11.2026 to 31.12.2026. |
| Atid Foundation tracks | Combat veterans (foundation track, distinct from MoD) | Per-track grant; check current cycle |
| IMPACT! (FIDF) | Former combat / combat-support soldiers, socioeconomic basis, applying within five years of discharge (or up to age 30 with 180+ war reserve days to 31.12.2025), starting year one or two | USD 5,000 per study year through the BA or a recognized certificate of a year or more (hand-engineers should confirm theirs counts), funded by a personal donor; 130 community hours each funded year. Register 1.3 to 31.5 every year. |

Claim every grant first and use the Pikadon for what remains or as a bridge, since it is the student's own money. Stress Mimadim's graduated model so the user budgets correctly for Year 1. Mimadim combines with track 45 (85% of that grant to the bank, 15% to the Pikadon). A first-year student eligible for both track 44 and Mimadim starts Mimadim from the second year.

### Step 4: Run the reservist checks (if served reserve duty since 7.10.2023)

Reservist money comes from three separate places: personal grants paid automatically by the IDF, tuition grants claimed from the IDF Reserve Aid Fund, and academic relief from the institution. **Read `references/reservist-benefits.md` before quoting a percentage**, because official pages disagree on some past-year terms.

| Benefit | Who qualifies | What it pays |
|---|---|---|
| Personal-expenses grant | 8+ Order 8 days to 31.12.2025. In 2026: 40+ days | NIS 1,100, then daily rates from day 41. Automatic, not student-specific |
| Family grant | Reservist with a child under 14 | NIS 2,000, during the war |
| **Tuition grant תשפ"ו** | 50+ days from 23.10.2025 to 30.9.2026 (until 31.12.2025 Order 8 only, then all reserve days) | By unit tier, % of university BA tuition: A+ 100, A 85, B 65, C and D 30, E 22. Apply 1.8.2026 to 30.6.2027 |
| Tuition grant תשפ"ה, תשפ"ד | Order 8: 50+ days (27.10.2024 to 30.9.2025; one page also counts תע"מ), 60+ days (7.10.2023 to 30.9.2024) | Combat array 100% (up to NIS 11,653, NIS 11,296). Other units 30% or 33%, sources differ. **Apply by 31.12.2026** |
| Private lessons | 21+ days | Up to NIS 2,000-3,000 by year and tier, against receipts. Through 31.12.2025, including early תשפ"ו: **by 31.12.2026**. From 1.1.2026: within one year of each event |
| PEREACH supplement | PEREACH tutor with 10+ active days | NIS 2,600, plus NIS 900 for an active reservist. Automatic |
| Credit-point relief | Set per academic year | BA cap 10 credits across the degree, MA up to 4. Via the reserve coordinator |

No תשפ"ז tuition-grant terms were published as of 23.11.2026. Do not project the תשפ"ו days or percentages onto תשפ"ז.

**Order rule:** a student who holds or may still apply for Mimadim, or is entitled to an MoD periphery scholarship, uses it first. **Never tell them to cancel Mimadim to take the reservist grant.** The Fund pays only a difference up to the grant ceiling, and only the Fund decides whether one exists. Anyone not entitled to either goes to the Fund. For a past year, ask the MoD (*5266) whether Mimadim or a periphery scholarship can still pay it, and **file with the Fund by 31.12.2026 without waiting for that answer**. Declare every scholarship, since the Fund's page and regulations differ on institutional ones.

Surface this step before Step 6 (olim) for any reservist user.

### Step 5: Statutory tracks for bereaved families, wounded soldiers, and evacuees

Three distinct statutory pipelines, each with its own intake and not addressed elsewhere:

| Track | Eligibility | What it pays |
|---|---|---|
| יתומי מערכות ישראל (orphans of fallen soldiers) and bereaved siblings | Statutory under חוק משפחות חיילים שנספו במערכה | Tuition support and stipends via Misrad HaBitachon Family & Commemoration Branch (אגף משפחות, הנצחה ומורשת); separate budget line from Mimadim/Periphery |
| נכי צה"ל (wounded soldiers) | Statutory under חוק הנכים (תגמולים ושיקום) | Tuition + rehabilitation support via the Rehabilitation Branch (אגף השיקום); start with the user's case manager |
| Evacuee (מפונים) tracks | Residents of עוטף עזה / north (Kiryat Shmona, Metula, Shlomi, etc.) evacuated since 7.10.2023 who became students 2024-2026 | Heznek La'Atid (heznek.org) and university emergency funds; amounts vary, so confirm with the institution |

For bereaved and wounded users, start with their case manager at אגף משפחות or אגף השיקום. The MoD discharged-soldiers division (hachvana) also offers them benefits. An orphan cannot use benefits in the same field from both divisions, or the Pikadon for a purpose the Family Branch funds, so compare before choosing. Route the user to the right office rather than listing per-case amounts.

### Step 9: Specialized tracks

Surface only if the profile matches:

- **Weizmann promotes-women-in-science** - aggregates national doctoral + postdoctoral-abroad fellowships.
- **Mifal HaPais athlete track** - for athletes on the Israeli national representative team.
- **University faculty-specific awards** - pattern only; instruct the user to check the target faculty's scholarships page after applying for institutional awards.
- **PEREACH reservist supplement** - NIS 2,600 for 10+ active days, plus NIS 900 for an active reservist (Step 4). Automatic.

### Step 10: Rank by deadline and recommend an application order

Once the possible matches are identified, rank by:

1. Imminent deadlines (anything closing in the next 30 days first)
2. Grant size (larger grants first within the same deadline tier)
3. Effort required (low-friction applications before essay-heavy foundation applications)

### Step 11: Surface the disqualifiers and stacking rules

A few rules cut across multiple tracks. Surface them before the user applies:

- Only one of MoD periphery tracks 44, 45, 46. Track 46 cannot combine with Mimadim. Combat reservists entitled to full funding are excluded from all three. Combat-support reservists who received 30% are topped up to 100% (44, 45) or 50% (46). For other tiers, ask *5266.
- The reservist tuition grant and Mimadim cannot both pay for the same tuition. A student who holds or may still apply for Mimadim uses it first and does not cancel it. Anyone not entitled goes to the Fund.
- MilGo has its own aid caps (Step 2), so check how other grants count.
- Mimadim stacks with private and volunteer scholarships with no need to report them. Other government scholarships reduce it, **except MilGo**: CHE and the MoD cancelled that offset on 18.5.2026.
- The Pikadon is the user's own money. An orphan who takes the Family Branch benefit cannot use it for the same purpose.
- Most foundation grants (Rashi, ISEF) require declaring all other received grants in the application; under-declaration risks revocation.
- PEREACH commitments (about 114 hours, 130 on the enhanced track) are real and audited. Don't recommend it to a student who can't realistically commit.

## Gotchas

1. **Mimadim LiLimudim is NOT flat 100% tuition.** It funds 3 study years, or 4 for engineering, medicine or architecture, refunding up to 85% each year but the last (NIS 10,372.55 on the NIS 12,203 ceiling in תשפ"ז), then topping up the withheld 15% in the final year. It is a refund, so the student fronts the tuition. At or below the ceiling about 15% stays out of pocket until the final year. Above the ceiling the excess is never refunded. Always state the model explicitly.
2. **Adams Fellowship excludes humanities and social sciences.** Despite the institution name "Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities," the Adams call covers exact and life sciences, mathematics, computer science, information science and engineering. Philosophy and sociology fall outside the fields the call lists. Route humanities/social-sciences PhDs to the Alix de Rothschild Fellowship.
3. **Never advise cancelling Mimadim to take the reservist tuition grant.** A student who holds or may still apply for Mimadim uses it first, and the Fund pays only a difference.
4. **SHEFI is not a scholarship.** It's the Ministry of Education's Psychological Counseling Service. The socioeconomic scholarship is MilGo, run by CHE and PEREACH, with 4 levels: NIS 12,480 / 10,000 / 7,500 / 5,000. Do not conflate them.
5. **Mahar, Achi and Masa Harhava are not confirmed scholarships.** Olim who ask for them by name should go to the Student Authority or the NBN higher-education page.
6. **Only one MoD periphery track.** Track 44 delays Mimadim to year two, track 45 combines with it, and track 46 cannot. Compare the whole degree, not the first year.
7. **The Rothschild USD 75,000/year is a CEILING on OTHER funding, not a cap on the Rothschild itself.** An applicant with USD 80,000/year in counted funding cannot apply. A Fellow who crosses the line during the fellowship loses the primary award but keeps travel and family support. Research grants, conference travel, health insurance and employee benefits do not count.
8. **MK Israel Foundation is unverified.** A user may ask about it by name based on older guides. Do NOT confirm its existence, direct them to verify via the institution's financial aid office before relying on it.
9. **Bereaved and wounded students have two offices.** Their branch (אגף משפחות or אגף השיקום) and the MoD discharged-soldiers division both offer benefits. Orphans cannot take the same field from both, or use the Pikadon for what the Family Branch funds.
10. **University tuition is NOT a flat NIS 12,203.** That is the university figure for תשפ"ז (NIS 12,017 for תשפ"ו). Actual tuition varies by program. When estimating coverage gaps, use the student's own payment statement.

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Student missed the May-September window for the upcoming academic year

Most major scholarship cycles are annual and the missed cohort is genuinely closed. Six fallbacks: (a) the Pikadon (if eligible) doesn't have a cycle, it can be used at any point in the post-discharge window; (b) PEREACH runs from September to around the end of November, so a "missed" May-September student may still get it; (c) reservist tuition grants have long claim windows (Step 4), and the personal grants are automatic; (d) university merit scholarships sometimes have rolling deadlines for late-admit students; (e) some foundations open winter rounds for spring-semester start; (f) Mimadim תשפ"ז is open to 31.8.2027 for those eligible, including olim defined as new immigrants during service. Be honest about which scholarships are off the table for this year.

### Issue: Student is in a periphery yishuv but for fewer than 5 of 6 years

Tracks 45 and 46 require 5 of the 6 years. Track 44 depends on studying at a priority-area institution, so check it. Other routes: (a) Rashi Katzir, asking whether a direct track is open, (b) PBC/MALAG outstanding-doctoral-from-periphery (for PhDs), (c) ISEF (which weighs periphery as a factor but doesn't require it), (d) the reservist tuition grant if the user served reserve duty (no residence gate).

### Issue: Student is a humanities PhD and asks about Adams Fellowship

Adams does not cover humanities. Route them to: (a) the Alix de Rothschild Fellowship, if the research proposal is approved (distinct from Yad Hanadiv), through the institution by 5.1.2027; (b) Yad Hanadiv Rothschild for a first postdoc abroad (does cover humanities); (c) PBC/MALAG outstanding-doctoral-from-periphery if eligible.

### Issue: Combat-veteran reservist qualifies for both the reservist tuition grant and Mimadim LiLimudim

This is not a free choice. A student who holds or may still apply for Mimadim uses it first and does not cancel it. Only the Fund decides whether a difference remains, so ask it (WhatsApp 052-3964584). For a past year, file by 31.12.2026 without waiting for the MoD. See `references/reservist-benefits.md`.

### Issue: Student wants to combine multiple scholarships beyond their actual cap

Some foundations cap stacked grants. Tell the user to declare all received grants in each application.
