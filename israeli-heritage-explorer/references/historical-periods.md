# Israeli Historical Periods -- Search Guide

**What this file is.** These are SEARCH TERMS grouped by period, not a historical
reference. The period brackets are a convenience for building `start_date` /
`end_date` clauses; they are not how NLI classifies its holdings, and the
boundaries are conventions that sources disagree about. Do not quote the dates or
the event framings below to a user as established fact. Where a user needs a
historical claim, take it from the sources the search returns, not from here.

**Search in more than one vocabulary.** NLI is a national library: it holds
Arabic-language newspapers, Islamic manuscripts, Ottoman-era material and
collections documenting non-Jewish communities alongside Jewish and Zionist
material. Many events are catalogued under several names, and a search that uses
only one community's terminology retrieves only one part of the holdings. Where a
topic has more than one common name, search all of them. This is a RECALL
problem before it is anything else.

## Ottoman period (search range 1517-1917/18)

### Key Search Terms
- English: `Palestine`, `Ottoman`, `Holy Land`, `Yishuv`, `Jaffa`, `Jerusalem`
- Hebrew: `ארץ ישראל`, `ירושלים`, `יפו`, `היישוב הישן`
- Arabic: `فلسطين`, `القدس`, `يافا`. `Yishuv` denotes the Jewish community only, so a
  term list built from it alone will miss the majority population of late-Ottoman
  Palestine and everything catalogued about it.

### Notable Events & Topics
- First Aliyah, conventionally 1882-1903: agricultural settlements, Petah Tikva, Rishon LeZion
- Second Aliyah, conventionally 1904-1914: kibbutz movement, Hebrew revival, Tel Aviv founding (1909)
- WWI impact on Palestine: the locust plague, wartime deportations including from Tel Aviv and Jaffa, and the British conquest

The "aliyah waves" scheme is a historiographical convention and every one of its
boundaries is given differently by different sources. Use the ranges to build a
date clause, not to date an event.

### Collection Tips
- Land ownership records (tabu/kushan documents)
- Sharia court records
- Photographs by American Colony photographers
- Maps by PEF (Palestine Exploration Fund)

## British rule (search range 1917-1948)

The label matters here. 1917 is the Ottoman military defeat and the Balfour
Declaration, not the start of the Mandate: the territory was under British
military administration first, and civil Mandate administration began in 1920,
with the Mandate itself confirmed and brought into legal force over the following
years. Do not tell a user researching Mandate government records that the Mandate
began in 1917; for the 1917-1920 material, search the military administration.

### Key Search Terms
- English: `Palestine`, `Mandate`, `Balfour`, `White Paper`, `Haganah`, `Irgun`, `OETA`, `military administration`
- Hebrew: `מנדט`, `פלשתינה`, `הכרזת בלפור`, `הספר הלבן`, `הגנה`
- Arabic: `الانتداب`, `فلسطين`

### Notable Events & Topics
- Balfour Declaration (1917); British military administration 1917-1920; civil Mandate administration from 1920
- Third and Fourth Aliyah, conventionally 1919-1923 and 1924-1929 (sources vary)
- Fifth Aliyah, conventionally 1929-1939 (some sources give 1932-1938): large-scale Central and Eastern European immigration, including German Jews fleeing Nazism, and substantially Polish as well
- 1936-1939 Arab Revolt
- WWII and Holocaust rescue efforts
- 1947 UN Partition Plan, 1948 British withdrawal

### Collection Tips
- Administrative documents in English (Mandate government records)
- Immigration certificates and ship manifests
- Underground press (Hebrew, Yiddish)
- Survey of Palestine maps (detailed topographic surveys)
- Herbert Samuel and other High Commissioner papers

## Early statehood (search range 1948-1967)

### Key Search Terms
- English: `Israel`, `independence`, `War of Independence`, `1948 War`, `1948 Arab-Israeli War`, `mass immigration`, `development towns`
- Hebrew: `עצמאות`, `מלחמת השחרור`, `מלחמת העצמאות`, `עלייה המונית`, `מעברות`, `עיירות פיתוח`
- Arabic: `النكبة`, `حرب 1948`

The 1948 war is catalogued under several names. A search using only the Israeli
national terms will return nothing from NLI's Arabic-language holdings on the
same events, so run the alternatives too when the user's question is about the
period rather than about Israeli national memory specifically.

### Notable Events & Topics
- Declaration of Independence (May 14, 1948)
- 1948 War and armistice agreements
- Mass immigration from Yemen and Iraq. Note the naming trap: "Magic Carpet" is a
  press coinage, and the Hebrew catalogue uses the operational names (`כנפי נשרים`
  for the Yemen airlift, `עזרא ונחמיה` for Iraq), so an English-only search on the
  popular name will under-retrieve
- Ma'abarot (transit camps) and development towns
- Sinai Campaign (1956)
- Eichmann Trial (1961)
- National Water Carrier (HaMovil HaArtzi), completed in the mid-1960s

### Collection Tips
- Government publications and Knesset protocols
- IDF archives (partial access)
- Immigrant absorption documentation
- Photographs by Government Press Office (GPO)
- Radio broadcasts (Kol Yisrael)

## Modern period (search range 1967-present)

### Key Search Terms
- English: `Six-Day War`, `Yom Kippur`, `peace process`, `Oslo`, `intifada`, `settlements`. Subject headings vary for the last of these; NLI also catalogues under neutral or alternative vocabulary, and Arabic material uses `مستوطنات` and `الانتفاضة`
- Hebrew: `מלחמת ששת הימים`, `מלחמת יום הכיפורים`, `הסכמי אוסלו`, `התנחלויות`

### Notable Events & Topics
- Six-Day War (1967) and the resulting occupation and administration of territory captured in it
- Yom Kippur War (1973)
- Camp David Accords (1978) and peace with Egypt
- First Lebanon War (1982)
- First Intifada (1987-1993)
- Oslo Accords (1993)
- Second Intifada (2000-2005)
- Gaza disengagement (2005)
- Contemporary oral history projects

### Collection Tips
- Oral history recordings (National Sound Archive)
- Contemporary photography collections
- Academic research publications
- Cultural documentation projects
- Newspaper digitization projects (historical Hebrew press)
